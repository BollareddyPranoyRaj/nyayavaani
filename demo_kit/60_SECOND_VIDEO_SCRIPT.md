# 🎬 60-Second Viral Hackathon Demo Video Script
**Target Award Category:** Best 60-Second Demo & Crowd Favourite  
**Video Tone:** Fast-paced, high empathy, high urgency, razor-sharp technical execution.

---

### ⏱️ Video Breakdown (Total: 59 Seconds)

#### [00:00 - 00:08] The Hook (High Emotion / Relatability)
* **Visual:** Close-up of phone screen receiving a WhatsApp call labeled "CBI Officer - Digital Arrest" or a simulation graphic.
* **Speaker (Voiceover / On-Camera):**  
  > *"Every 4 minutes in India, a common citizen in a Tier-2 or Tier-3 town gets scammed, extorted, or threatened. When they panic-call for help, they don't know Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) legal sections, and they speak in code-mixed Hinglish or Telugu over noisy phone lines."*

---

#### [00:08 - 00:20] The Reveal: Meet NyayaVaani
* **Visual:** Cut to screen recording of the **NyayaVaani Tactical Dispatch Dashboard** (`http://localhost:8000`). Show the glowing live waveform and audio telemetry.
* **Speaker:**  
  > *"Meet **NyayaVaani**—an AI-powered telephonic first-responder built on Gnani.ai's voice models. Let's simulate a live citizen call in distress under extreme street and telephonic noise."*
* **Action:** Click on **"Case 1: Digital Arrest Scam"** preset or click **"Live Mic Call"**.

---

#### [00:20 - 00:35] The Gnani AI Powerhouse (Prisma + Evon)
* **Visual:** Show the real-time transcription appearing with the badge `hi-IN (Hinglish) • Auto-Detected`. Highlight the traffic noise indicator (`8.4 dB Noise`).
* **Speaker:**  
  > *"Even through 8kHz mobile line noise, **Gnani Prisma v3.0** flawlessly transcribes code-mixed Hindi-English verbatim. Next, **Gnani Evon 30B** acts as a legal triage engine: it identifies extortion, maps the crime to Section 308 and 319 of the new BNS 2023, plus Section 66D of the IT Act, and triggers the Golden Hour financial freeze alert."*

---

#### [00:35 - 00:48] Empathetic Voice & Instant Legal Dossier (Timbre + FIR)
* **Visual:** Point to the **Timbre Voice Player** and click play. Show the official PDF FIR dossier opening up with QR code and BNSS Form 154 layout.
* **Audio:** Let Timbre's synthesized voice play: *"रमेश जी, बिल्कुल शांत हो जाइए। आपकी शिकायत दर्ज हो गई है..."*
* **Speaker:**  
  > *"**Gnani Timbre v3.0** speaks back calmly in their native tongue with natural pauses, reassuring the victim, while NyayaVaani auto-generates a stamped, legal-grade bilingual FIR PDF ready to submit to the Thana or Cyber Portal."*

---

#### [00:48 - 00:59] The Call to Action & Conclusion
* **Visual:** Quick montage of the Telugu farmland case and the dual-screen dashboard. Cut to the two teammates on webcam smiling.
* **Speaker:**  
  > *"From rural farmers to college students, NyayaVaani brings equal justice to 1.4 billion citizens in their mother tongue. Built for the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge by Bollareddy Pranoy Raj and teammate. Thank you, Gnani AI!"*
* **On-Screen Text:**  
  * `#GnaniAI #GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge`  
  * Built with: Prisma v3.0 | Evon 30B | Timbre v3.0  
  * GitHub repo link on screen.

### 💡 Pro-Tips for Maximum Reach on Social Media:
1. **Record in 1080p 60fps** (use OBS Studio or Loom with camera bubble in bottom corner).
2. **Add Auto-Captions** (use CapCut, Premiere, or Descript) — 80% of LinkedIn users watch videos on mute!
3. **Keep the energy high** — no long pauses, seamless transitions.
