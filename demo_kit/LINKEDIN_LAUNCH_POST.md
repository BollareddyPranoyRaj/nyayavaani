What happens when a 55-year-old farmer or a tier-2 college student gets targeted by a "Digital Arrest" scam or extortion? 

They panic. They call helplines from noisy streets, speaking a frantic mix of Hindi, Telugu, and English. 
They don't know the new Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS 2023) sections. 
And before anyone can help, the "Golden Hour" to freeze the scammed funds is lost.

Together with my teammate, we asked: **What if India's citizens had a 24x7 voice-first legal guardian that speaks their mother tongue, cuts through chaotic telephone noise, and drafts an airtight police complaint in 60 seconds?**

Introducing ⚖️ **NyayaVaani (न्यायवाणी / న్యాయవాణి)** — An AI Vernacular Telephonic First-Responder for Bharat, built for the **#GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge** powered by **Gnani.ai**.

Here is how we leveraged Gnani's state-of-the-art Indic speech & LLM models:

🎙️ **Gnani Prisma v3.0 (STT):** Handles raw 8kHz mobile audio with severe ambient street, traffic, and wind noise. Its multi-lingual engine auto-detects code-mixed Hinglish and Telugu seamlessly (`indic-auto`).

🧠 **Gnani Evon 30B (Reasoning LLM):** Acts as a legal triage copilot. It classifies the modus operandi, maps the offense directly to the statutory sections of the newly enacted **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS 2023)** (e.g., Sec 308 for extortion, Sec 319 for impersonation, and Sec 66D IT Act), and calculates the Golden Hour emergency window.

🔊 **Gnani Timbre v3.0 (Neural TTS):** Delivers empathetic, calm spoken guidance back to the panicked victim using natural audio pauses (`<pauses>`), reassuring them in their native dialect while actions are underway.

📄 **Instant BNSS Compliant FIR Dossier:** Automatically generates a bilingual (English + Vernacular), digitally verifiable PDF complaint formatted to BNSS Section 173 standards, ready to WhatsApp to the Thana SHO or upload to the 1930 Cyber Cell portal.

Over 90% of our country struggles with legal literacy and language barriers during distress. NyayaVaani proves that with cutting-edge Indic voice AI, justice and emergency response can be democratized for all 1.4 billion citizens.
