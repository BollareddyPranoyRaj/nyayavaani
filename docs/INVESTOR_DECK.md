## Slide 1: Executive Summary
* **Vision:** Equal, instant legal protection and cybercrime first-response for 1.4 billion citizens in their mother tongue over a regular phone call.
* **Core Technology:** Built on **Gnani AI**'s foundation speech and LLM stack (Prisma v3.0 STT, Evon 30B Reasoning LLM, Timbre v3.0 Neural TTS).
* **Key Innovation:** Zero-app telephonic triage that listens to code-mixed dialects under 8kHz mobile line noise, maps crimes to the new **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS 2023)**, and auto-generates stamped **BNSS Form 154 FIR dockets**.

---

## Slide 2: The Macro Problem
1. **The Cyber Extortion Wave:** Over ₹1,750 Crores lost in India in 2024–2026 to "Digital Arrest" and fake courier scams.
2. **Language & Noise Barrier:** 95% of Tier-2/3 victims call helplines from chaotic streets, speaking fragmented Hinglish or Telugu that conventional voice bots fail to parse.
3. **Legal Literacy Gap:** The colonial IPC was replaced by BNS 2023. Neither citizens nor junior police desk officers know the exact legal sections to register during the critical 2-hour **"Golden Hour"**.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution (NyayaVaani)
* **24x7 Universal Helpline:** Works over standard telephonic and cellular voice networks (no smartphone or 4G data required).
* **De-escalation & Triage:** Calms down panicked callers in their regional dialect.
* **Automated Police FIR:** Compiles a legally certified, digitally verifiable bilingual complaint docket ready for immediate police and cyber cell dispatch.
